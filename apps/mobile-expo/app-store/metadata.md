Connect to your xopc gateway and bring your workspace with you. Capture notes and files, continue conversations with your AI assistant, review tasks, and manage scheduled work.

Choose the tools you use: attach a photo, record a voice message, listen to an answer, or share content into your workspace. Review configured AI and speech services before sharing your content, and manage permission in the app.

5. Open a sample note, create a task, and start an assistant conversation. Review the named gateway services and choose “Agree and continue” before submitting content.
