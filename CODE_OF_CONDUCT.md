# xopc Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making every official xopc community space welcoming, respectful, and safe for people of every background and experience level. This applies to the repository, GitHub Discussions, Discord, WeChat groups, events, and private communication made in a project role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep conversations relevant and move reusable conclusions into public, searchable project records.
- Protect privacy. Do not repost another person's private messages, personal information, credentials, or private configuration without permission.
- Respect maintainers' and volunteers' time; community support is best-effort.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, spam, scams, or repeated off-topic promotion.
- Publishing private information, credentials, security vulnerabilities, or private correspondence without permission.
- Impersonating project members or misrepresenting an unofficial channel as an official xopc community.

## Enforcement

Moderators may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove someone from an official community space. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made without retaliation against good-faith reporters.

To report a problem privately, email [lyxopc.ai@gmail.com](mailto:lyxopc.ai@gmail.com) with the channel, relevant links or screenshots, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be shared only with the people needed to review and address them. Security vulnerabilities must instead use [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/xopcai/xopc/security/advisories/new).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies whenever someone represents xopc or participates in an official xopc space. It does not make unrelated third-party communities official or place them under xopc moderation.
