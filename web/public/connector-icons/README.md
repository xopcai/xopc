# Connector icon sources

These assets are shown for identification in the xopc connector catalog. Product names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners.

- `notion.svg` and `slack.svg`: copied from the local OpenHuman repository (`app/src/assets/icons/notion.svg` and `app/src-tauri/recipes/slack/icon.svg`).
- `github.svg`, `google-drive.svg`, and `linear.svg`: downloaded from the Composio logo service used by OpenHuman (`https://logos.composio.dev/api/{slug}`).
- `gmail.svg` and `google-calendar.svg`: local brand SVGs maintained with the built-in connector catalog.
- The remaining product SVGs (including Airtable, ClickUp, and Composio) are downloaded from the Composio logo service (`https://logos.composio.dev/api/{slug}`).
- `brave-search.svg`: official Brave icon from `https://brave.com/static-assets/images/brave-logo-sans-text.svg`.
- `playwright.svg`: official Playwright logo from `microsoft/playwright.dev` (`static/img/playwright-logo.svg`).
- `fetch.svg`, `filesystem.svg`, `memory.svg`, `sequential-thinking.svg`, and `time.svg`: xopc-owned functional icons for generic MCP utilities without a distinct product brand.
